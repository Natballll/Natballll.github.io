Interning at Encyclopaedia Britannica

Summer 2026

At Britannica, I prepared multimedia articles in anticipation of the launch of NASA's Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope. This included the main text article on the subject, as well as visual interactives on the telescope's scientific objectives and how its capabilities compare with other space telescopes like the Hubble Space Telescope. I also wrote and produced a video on the topic for social media with Adobe Premiere Pro and After Effects. Other projects included writing on the 1969 moon landing and extrasolar planet discoveries for the Today in History newsletter, and a long-form article on the largest labor uprising in United States history.

Comics for Science Communication

Spring 2026

In Comics for Health and Science Communication, I learned about designing comics for patient education, research and advocacy. My final project was a 10-page comic about a recent astrophysics discovery by University of Chicago undergraduates. To collect information and quotes for my piece, I conducted three interviews with members of the team. Most challenging and rewarding was to weave their various perspectives into a larger narrative, and what emerged was a story about science that was all about one generation nurturing the next. The piece is linked here.

Interning at University Communications

Winter-Spring 2025

My capstone project at the University's Central Communications Department was a feature story on the University's Special Collections, capturing the center's work to archive all kinds of history. To prepare for the story, I visited the Collections to survey the archives and conducted an interview with the Collections' director. While not quite science communication, this project certainly involved communicating about scientists. Particularly memorable was writing about the experience of leafing through Nobel Prize Winner for Physics Subrahmanyan Chandrasekhar's papers, which captured his thoughts as a young, aspiring researcher. The piece is linked here.
